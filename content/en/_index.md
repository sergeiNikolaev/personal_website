Welcome to my site. My name is Sergei. I am from Saint Petersburg, Russia.

On this site I share my portfolio and also posts with information, I find useful. I decided to create it, since I realized that my experience cannot be covered by a simple CV. In certain cases, more details and descriptions have to be given to explain the nature of the considered problem and the proposed solution. In addition to that, sites structures are highly flexible compared with personal pages in social networks like LinkedIn.

My professional interests include physically based modeling in biomechanics, computational biology, and also three-dimensional point clouds and mesh processing. I’ve graduated from the [Saint Petersburg State University](https://english.spbu.ru), [faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics](https://math.spbu.ru), Russia. In my diploma thesis, I described a creation of a multilayer mass-spring model and procedure that simulates an implant or an expander extension under soft tissues. It was supposed to predict the result of a surgical procedure. Several years later I defended my PhD at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.unistra.fr), France. The topic was dedicated to the estimation of boundary conditions around a liver organ. After that I worked as a postdoctoral researcher in the computational biology domain at the [University of Montpellier](https://www.umontpellier.fr/en/), France. The project was related to a model that describes an embryo development process. More details can be found on the [research]({{< ref "research/index.md" >}}) page.

Talking about personal interests, I enjoy jogging, and several times I participated in challenges related to sports orienteering. I love traveling, though more like a tourist, and I have visited many places, particularly in France. My another hobby is cooking; however, I prefer to prepare plates where the taste is obtained by mixing the specific ingredients and not by using complex cooking conditions or environment. Also I am performing learning in different domains. For example, recently I got known with the concept of [deliberate practice](https://jamesclear.com/deliberate-practice-theory) and physical exercises based on [kinesiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinesiology). This has shifted my understanding on how we have to train our brain and our muscles. By the way, [talent is overrated](https://geoffcolvin.com/books/talent-is-overrated); thus, it is possible for everybody to improve their abilities in everything.

## My philosophy

Health is one of the vital parts in our lives. Nonetheless, nowadays there are still a lot of diseases that cannot be treated. In addition to that, many medical procedures are done manually and require very skilled specialists, the training of whom takes a lot of time. However, using modern technologies, particularly imaging modalities, scientific knowledge, and a possibility to model human body and various impacts on biological processes, there is a chance to improve the situation significantly.

Therefore, the general purpose of my work is to treat recorded imaging data, point clouds and after construct models or numerical systems that will provide us with better knowledge related to the underlined biological and biomechanical processes. During my career, as an engineer or a researcher, I participated in several projects related to simulation in biomechanics and computational biology. They are aimed to construct mathematical models or numerical software for analyzing the nature of the process and predicting the behavior of a biological system.
